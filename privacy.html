Mom's Accounting: privacy

Mom's Accounting runs on each firm's own office Mac. The firm's books, papers and client details stay on that Mac, and in the firm's own Google Drive if the firm connects one for its backup copy.

When someone signs in with Google, Google sends them to this page, and this page sends them straight on to their firm's Mac. No client data passes through this page. It keeps nothing, sets no cookies, and has no analytics.

From Google, the app asks only for the person's name, email address and picture, to know who is signed in. The Drive backup asks only for the files the app itself makes. Nobody else, including the app's maker, receives any of it.

To stop the app's access, remove it in your Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps and services.
